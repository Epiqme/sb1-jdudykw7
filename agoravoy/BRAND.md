# Agoravoy Brand Spec — single source of truth

Locked by Epiq on 2026-07-13. Every video, card, and graphic follows this. If a doc conflicts with this file (old Anton references in HANDOFF.md / VIDEO-QUALITY-PLAYBOOK.md), THIS file wins.

## Colors (same as agoravoy.com)

| Use | Color | Hex | ASS (BGR) | ffmpeg |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brand red — accents, keyword pops, URL box | red | `#E10A0A` | `&H000A0AE1` | `0xE10A0A` |
| Ocean blue — end-card background | blue | `#00A8D8` | `&H00D8A800` | `0x00A8D8` |
| Text | white | `#FFFFFF` | `&H00FFFFFF` | `white` |
| Caption outline | black | `#000000` | `&H00000000` | `black` |

No other colors on any graphic. No green, ever (old HeyGen renders had green overlays — always replace).

## Font

**Bebas Neue** — `agoravoy/assets/BebasNeue.ttf` (OFL). Applies to captions, title slams, end cards, and any on-screen text in HeyGen prompts. All text UPPERCASE.
(Anton is retired; `assets/Anton-Regular.ttf` kept only so old project files still render.)

## Captions — style "B3: no box, heavy outline"

Word-pop chunks of 2-3 words, generated by `pipeline/make_ass.py` (these are its defaults):

- Font: Bebas Neue **104px** (at 1080x1920)
- White fill, **black outline 10**, no shadow, no background box
- Keywords in brand-red **letters** (`&H000A0AE1`), not boxed
- Position: bottom-center, `MarginV 500` (inside the universal 900x1400 safe zone), margins L60/R120
- Fade-in 50ms per pop (`\fad(50,0)`)

## Title slam (series card, e.g. "DEAL SCAN #1")

- Bebas Neue 96px, white text in a solid brand-red box (ASS BorderStyle 3, outline 14)
- Top-center (`Alignment 8`, MarginV 500 from top), shown ~2.5s during the opening B-roll
- Also produced by `make_ass.py` via `--title`

## End card — style "B: Clean" (3.2s, closes every video)

Ocean-blue `#00A8D8` background, three drawtext lines in Bebas Neue, all centered:

1. `AGORAVOY.COM` — 120px white in a red `#E10A0A` box, boxborderw 48, y=780
2. `BOOK VIRGIN.` — 58px white, y=1110
3. `BOARD WITH FRIENDS.` — 58px white, y=1195

No series pill on the end card. Exact command in `pipeline/video-build-recipe.md` §4.

## Safe zones (1080x1920)

Keep every graphic inside the centered **900x1400** box: ~250px clear top, ~270px+ clear bottom, 60px left, 120px right.
