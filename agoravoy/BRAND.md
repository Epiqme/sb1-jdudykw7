## Fonts

- **Wordmark/logo: Anton** — `agoravoy/assets/Anton-Regular.ttf` — matches the agoravoy.com header exactly.
- **Captions & title slams: Bebas Neue** — `agoravoy/assets/BebasNeue.ttf` (Epiq's pick for video text). All caption text UPPERCASE.

## Captions — style "B3: no box, heavy outline"

Word-pop chunks of 2-3 words, generated by `pipeline/make_ass.py` (these are its defaults):

- Font: Bebas Neue **104px** (at 1080x1920)
- White fill, **black outline 10**, no shadow, no background box
- Keywords in brand-red **letters** (`&H000A0AE1`), not boxed
- Position: bottom-center, `MarginV 500` (inside the universal 900x1400 safe zone), margins L60/R120
- Fade-in 50ms per pop (`\fad(50,0)`)

## Title slam (series card, e.g. "DEAL SCAN #1") — style "T1: no box"

NO background box (Epiq: no red boxes anywhere).

- Bebas Neue 110px, white text, heavy black outline 10, no shadow (matches the caption style)
- Top-center (`Alignment 8`, MarginV 500 from top), shown ~2.5s during the opening B-roll
- Produced by `make_ass.py` via `--title`

## End card — FINAL, locked by Epiq 2026-07-13 (3.2s, closes every video)

Generated by `pipeline/make_endcard.py` (never hand-build it). Master PNG: `assets/endcard-master.png`.

- **White background** (matches the site header)
- **AGORAVOY wordmark**: Anton 140px, letter spacing 0.12em, upright, baseline y=940 — **AGORA in ocean `#00A8D8`, VOY in red `#E10A0A`**
- **agoravoy.com**: red lowercase Anton 32px, justified edge-to-edge with the wordmark, tucked at its base (baseline y=990)

No boxes, no shadow, no slant. Clip command in `pipeline/video-build-recipe.md` §4.

## Safe zones (1080x1920)

Keep every graphic inside the centered **900x1400** box: ~250px clear top, ~270px+ clear bottom, 60px left, 120px right.
